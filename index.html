Revision preview

Historical preview. Highlighted text is the deletion before an edit or the insertion after it; a caret marks other points. First edit opens the text immediately before the revision, and Last edit opens the text after the whole revision.

A note on the notation & implementation

Based on S-Notation Markup Rules, Technical Description, v1.0 (July 7, 2012), prepared by Eric Van Horenbeeck, Tom Pauwaert, Luuk Van Waes, and Mariëlle Leijten.

S-notation needs an edit history; a finished text alone cannot reveal its revisions. This demo captures edits made in this editor. Pasting a finished text is normal production until you revise it. Continuous typing or deleting is grouped into a single operation; moving the caret or generating output ends that group. Replacements are a deletion followed by an insertion. Browser undo and redo are recorded as new edits.

The engine counts only live text for positions, tracks active characters in nested insertions, and applies the PDF’s markup jumping rules. A break is associated with a revision, not with an elapsed-time pause. The ledger uses the final positions of the break and closing marker; later edits can move those points. Live recording starts when the page opens or Start over is clicked. Navigation time is the first observed caret or selection movement since the preceding edit. Imported ScriptLog timestamps retain their original relative times. The paper’s example has no timestamps, so those cells show a dash. Plain-text exports use subscripts for revision numbers. Literal braces, brackets, and pipes in your text are escaped with a backslash in exports. Input and history stay in memory and disappear on reload.
